## Capability outcome

The learner can construct multiple plausible, relevant, divergent, and internally consistent scenarios, then derive implications without treating scenarios as forecasts.

## Observable learning outcomes

1. Choose and defend a scenario method appropriate to the focal question.
2. Create four meaningfully different scenario logics from approved drivers and uncertainties.
3. Develop internally consistent narratives with actors, mechanisms, milestones, and signposts.
4. Detect wishful thinking, collapse toward a middle future, and contradictions.
5. Derive first-, second-, and third-order implications for named stakeholders.
6. Communicate uncertainty clearly without attaching invented probabilities.

## Learning sequence

| Unit | Instruction | Guided practice | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Method choice | Axes, morphological, archetypal, and participatory approaches | Match methods to five decision contexts | Method memo |

## Applied lab

Task: produce a four-scenario set for the system designed in VT-102.

Each scenario must include: one-sentence logic; 800–1,200-word narrative; named actors; causal chain; 3–5 milestones; early signposts; disconfirming evidence; stakeholder implications; opportunities; risks; and a consistency review.

Fault injection: a new regulatory shock arrives after draft one, one scenario contains a contradiction with its driver logic, and two narratives converge on the same outcome. The learner must revise without erasing the version trail.

## Holdout transfer task

Given an unseen driver matrix, outline two contrasting scenarios and produce a five-step implication cascade for one stakeholder in 75 minutes.

## Assessment rubric

| Dimension | Weight | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Method and logic | 15 | Method fits the decision and logic follows approved uncertainties |
| Plausibility and consistency | 25 | Causal mechanisms hold together; contradictions are resolved |
| Divergence and breadth | 15 | Futures differ materially and avoid preferred/middle-case collapse |
| Implications | 20 | Multi-order impacts are specific and stakeholder-linked |
| Signposts and evidence | 15 | Observable indicators preserve source and uncertainty boundaries |
| Communication and instruction adherence | 10 | Clear, engaging, non-predictive framing |

Critical failures: labeling a preferred scenario as certain, inventing probabilities, hiding internal contradictions, or removing material contrary evidence.

## Remediation

The learner receives a different driver architecture, rebuilds only the failed scenario components, and completes a fresh consistency test. Original narratives remain retained.

## Evidence manifest

Required: method memo, logic table, four narratives, driver-to-scenario trace, consistency matrix, red-team notes, implication maps, signpost register, revision history, holdout output, timestamps, retries, self-review, and independent QA decision.
