## Capability outcome

The learner can convert a signal portfolio into a defensible system of drivers, relationships, assumptions, and critical uncertainties suitable for scenario work.

## Observable learning outcomes

1. Consolidate signals into drivers without losing source lineage.
2. Map causal relationships, feedback loops, delays, dependencies, and affected actors.
3. Separate predetermined elements from uncertain drivers.
4. Rank uncertainties using impact, uncertainty, decision relevance, and independence.
5. Expose assumptions and identify evidence that would confirm or weaken them.
6. Select a scenario architecture without forcing complex change into convenient axes.

## Learning sequence

| Unit | Instruction | Guided practice | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. From signal to driver | Aggregation rules and traceability | Merge 18 signal cards into candidate drivers | Driver register |
| 2. System structure | Actors, stocks, flows, feedback, delays | Annotate a flawed system map | Corrected map |
| 3. Assumptions | Explicit and hidden assumptions | Run an assumption reversal exercise | Assumption ledger |
| 4. Uncertainty | Predetermined vs uncertain; impact and independence | Score and debate 10 drivers | Uncertainty matrix |
| 5. Architecture | Axes, morphological fields, archetypes | Compare three architecture choices | Architecture decision |
| 6. Indicators | Leading indicators and signposts | Define measurable watches | Indicator register |

## Applied lab

Task: transform the verified VT-101 signal portfolio into a driver system for a ten-year decision horizon.

Required outputs: 8–12 drivers; source lineage to every driver; actor map; relationship map; at least two reinforcing or balancing feedback loops; five explicit assumptions; top four critical uncertainties; and leading indicators.

Fault injection: two highly ranked drivers are correlated, a politically preferred driver lacks evidence, and a predetermined element is incorrectly scored as uncertain. The learner must identify and correct all three.

## Holdout transfer task

Given an unseen 10-signal pack, create a compact driver map and select two independent critical uncertainties in 60 minutes. Defend what was excluded.

## Assessment rubric

| Dimension | Weight | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Signal-to-driver lineage | 20 | Every driver is supported and traceable |
| Systems reasoning | 25 | Relationships, feedback, delays, and actors are coherent |
| Assumption discipline | 15 | Assumptions are explicit, testable, and challenged |
| Uncertainty selection | 20 | High-impact, genuinely uncertain, decision-relevant, sufficiently independent |
| Indicators and monitoring | 10 | Specific observable signposts with owners or sources |
| Communication and instruction adherence | 10 | Clear rationale, exclusions, and limitations |

Critical failures: deleting contrary evidence, fabricating causal support, or selecting scenario axes solely to produce a preferred conclusion.

## Remediation

The learner repairs the failed structure using a new signal pack and documents every changed driver, relationship, and uncertainty. Previous maps remain versioned.

## Evidence manifest

Required: driver register, lineage table, system map, assumption ledger, uncertainty matrix, architecture decision, indicator register, lab revision history, holdout output, timings, retries, self-review, and independent QA decision.
