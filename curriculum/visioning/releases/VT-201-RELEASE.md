## Scholar note

Strategy under uncertainty should be tested across multiple plausible futures. Wind-tunnelling compares each option against each scenario using criteria fixed before scoring. The result is not one timeless answer but a portfolio: no-regret actions, option-preserving moves, contingent actions tied to triggers, hedges against severe downside, and stops for actions that fail authority or risk limits. Backcasting begins with a defined desired state and works backward through milestones and dependencies. A monitored roadmap assigns owners, indicators, trigger thresholds, review dates, escalation paths, and kill criteria while preserving final owner control.

Retrieval checks:

1. Distinguish a no-regret action from a contingent action.
2. Explain why criteria must be fixed before scoring options.
3. State when an option should be stopped even if its average score is high.

## Exam and rubric

The sealed exam provides three scenarios and four unfamiliar options. In 105 minutes, build a stress-test matrix, classify the portfolio, and write a 90-day no-regret action plan. Scoring: option breadth 10; criteria integrity 15; cross-scenario test 25; portfolio robustness 20; backcast and roadmap 15; evidence and monitoring 10; defence 5. ## Remediation pack
